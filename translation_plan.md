# AI Song Translation: plan

Its own plan, separate from `projectplan.md`.

## The idea

Give an AI chat (for example Google AI Mode) a **translation guide** plus a song name, such as
"Hot Cross Buns". The guide explains how the Music Staff app stores music and what it can't do. The AI
replies with **exactly the app's save file** (`.music.json`): a full arrangement, simplified to fit the
app's limits. Paste that reply into the app and the song plays.

## How it's used

1. In the app: **Help ▸ Copy AI Translation Guide** puts the guide on the clipboard.
2. In the AI chat: paste the guide, then type the song's name after `SONG REQUEST:`.
3. Copy the AI's whole reply.
4. In the app: **File ▸ Paste Song from AI** (Ctrl+Shift+V), paste, **Open Song**. Then save it like any song.

If the reply has problems, the paste window explains them and **Copy for the AI** makes a message to
paste back to the AI so it can send a corrected file.

## Decisions (from the interview, 2026-10-17)

- **AI output:** the exact save file. No in-between format to convert.
- **Input to the AI:** just a song name.
- **Arrangement:** full arrangement: the AI chooses several instruments.
- **Things the app can't do** (sixteenth notes, triplets, repeats, pickup measures, time or key changes
  partway through...): the AI **simplifies** by fixed rules and **explains** what it changed in the
  file's `ai_notes`, which the paste window shows.
- **Length:** the whole song if it fits in about 64 measures; otherwise its best-known section.
- **The guide is generated by the app** from its own instrument list, limits and file format, so it can
  never be out of date. A copy is kept in `AI_TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md` (regenerate with
  `python translation_guide.py`; a test checks it's current).
- **Pasting:** the paste window finds the file inside the AI's reply (ignoring its chatter), fixes what's
  safe (and lists the fixes), and explains real problems in plain words.

## Why the exact save file needs care

The save file stores notes as **staff steps** (lines and spaces counted from the bottom line), which mean
different pitches in treble and bass clef, and transposing instruments store their *written* pitch. An
AI asked to calculate these will make mistakes, so the guide gives **lookup tables** (pitch ↔ step for
each clef, drum positions, each instrument's transposition in plain words) and a **complete worked
example** generated from real app data, so the example is guaranteed to load.

## Parts

| Part | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| a | `translation_guide.py`, `AI_TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md` | Builds the guide from the app's data |
| b | `song_import.py` | Finds, checks, fixes and loads the file in an AI's reply |
| c | `main.py` | File ▸ Paste Song, Help ▸ Copy AI Translation Guide |
| d | (a real trial) | Try Google AI Mode with "Hot Cross Buns"; improve the guide from what goes wrong |

## Known limits

- An AI may **misremember** a song, especially a less famous one; the result plays, but may not be
  quite the real tune.
- An AI may **decline** to reproduce some copyrighted songs.
- Very long arrangements can be **cut off** by the AI's reply length; the 64-measure limit and one-line
  notes keep replies short.
- Anything the app can't represent is approximated, and listed in the AI's notes.

## Trial log (calibration)

What real AI replies got wrong, and what changed in response. Real replies are songs, so they are
never added to the project; the tests use made-up tunes with the same mistakes.

| # | AI | What went wrong | Change made |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Google AI Mode | `"time_signature":,` came back empty (probably `[4, 4]` was taken for a citation and dropped) | Time signatures are written as text, `"4/4"` (file format version 6; `[4, 4]` still reads). Empty values are read as missing. |
| 1 | Google AI Mode | `"format": "Music Staff"` instead of `"music-staff"` | Guide says "exactly this text"; the importer corrects it. |
| 1 | Google AI Mode | Bass-clef staves (piano left hand, bass guitar) written with **treble-clef** steps, giving wrong notes far too low | Guide: separate treble and bass tables, by note name, saying which staves use each, with a warning example. Importer: a bass staff with notes below its range is converted as treble steps (same pitches), then leftovers move by octaves. |
| 1 | Google AI Mode | `"ai_notes"` was a list | Guide says one string; the importer joins a list. |
